How the GEO Audit Works

Our audit strips away the guesswork. We don't rely on theory — we aggressively test the primary language models to determine mathematically whether your firm is being recommended.

If you request a free audit, here is precisely what we check and how our data pipeline operates.

The Technical Breakdown

1

We Isolate Your Competitive Search Space

We do not run generic queries. We prompt the models using the exact conversational queries your prospective clients use (e.g., "Recommend a specialist commercial architect in Melbourne for a mid-tier development project").

2

We Scan the Big Three LLMs

We compile data simultaneously across ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), and Gemini (Google). We track exactly which firms are output in the responses, marking who dominates the recommendation share.

3

We Identify Hallucinated Citations

If a model recommends your competitor but cites inaccurate data (or hallucinates an advantage they don't have), we identify that gap. We analyse the trust signals that caused the model to favour them over your brand.

4

We Calculate Your Baseline Score

You receive a transparent metric out of 100 representing your total AI visibility. This gives you a clear, undeniable picture of exactly how much market share you are losing to generative search engines right now.

What Happens After the Audit?

We compile the data into a custom report and present it via a discovery call. We screen-share the raw responses from the models so you can see the proof with your own eyes.

There are no lock-in contracts and no aggressive sales pitches. If the data reveals you have a problem, we will offer our GEO Setup Package to correct it. If your AI visibility is already flawless, we'll simply let you know.

Let the data speak for itself.
