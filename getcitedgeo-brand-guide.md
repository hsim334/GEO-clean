## What We Do

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) agency. We help businesses get discovered, cited, and recommended by AI systems — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews.

**The core problem we solve:** Traditional SEO optimises for Google rankings. It does nothing for AI search. Most businesses are currently invisible to AI systems — and their customers are already using those systems to make decisions.

---

## Brand Voice

### The Character
Think of a sharp junior partner at a specialist consulting firm who genuinely loves their work. Confident, not arrogant. Direct, not aggressive. Expert, not jargon-heavy.

### Tone Attributes
- **Sharp** — Every sentence earns its place. No padding.
- **Authoritative** — Leads with data and insight, not opinion.
- **Controlled enthusiasm** — Genuine energy, not hype.
- **Plain language** — Smart clients don't need jargon. Explain GEO in terms they already understand.

### What We Sound Like
> "Most businesses score under 40/100 on a GEO audit. That means AI systems are actively skipping you."

> "SEO doesn't cover AI search. Different game. Different signals."

> "We'll show you your score for free. No fluff, just your data."

### What We Don't Sound Like
- "Leverage our holistic, end-to-end GEO solution to unlock synergistic digital transformation"
- "Are you ready to DOMINATE AI search???"
- "We're passionate about helping businesses grow online"

---

## Writing Rules

- **Australian English** — optimise, analyse, colour, behaviour (no exceptions)
- Short paragraphs — one idea per paragraph
- Lead with the insight, then explain
- "You/your" not "we/our" in client-facing copy
- No exclamation marks in client-facing copy
- Data first, opinion second
- Never use: "leverage", "synergy", "holistic approach", "end-to-end solution", "digital transformation"

## What We Never Promise

- Never guarantee "you will appear in ChatGPT responses"
- Never guarantee specific traffic or ranking outcomes
- Never use the word "guarantee" in relation to AI visibility results

**What we do promise:** measurable audit score improvement, best-practice implementation, honest reporting

---

## Target Audience

Business owners and marketing managers who:
- Know AI search is growing but don't know how to act on it
- Are already doing SEO but haven't considered AI citations
- Are problem-aware or solution-aware — not necessarily product-aware
- Are making decisions about digital investment

---

## Ad Creative Rules

- Always include a specific data point or real number — never generic claims
- Show don't tell — audit scores, competitor gaps, before/after
- Soft CTA only: "Book your free audit" or "See your score" — never hard sell
- Visual: screenshots, score visuals, and data outperform stock photography
- Hook must land within 125 characters (Meta primary text above the fold)
